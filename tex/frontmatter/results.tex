\begin{abstract}
Goldbach conjectured that every even integer $n\geq4$ is a sum of two primes.
This edition collects the statements that are established here together with
their proofs: the elementary characterisation of the even integers admitting
such a representation, the exact finite verification for $n\leq10^{8}$, and the
range $n\leq12$ that has been formally verified in Lean.  Open proof
programmes, failed strengthenings, and detailed computational audits are
maintained separately in the routes companion and the full working edition.
\end{abstract}
